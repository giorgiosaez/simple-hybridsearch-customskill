Practical energy improvements

A facilities team can reduce energy use by measuring a baseline before changing
equipment settings. Compare occupied and unoccupied periods, review unusual
overnight demand, and check that heating and cooling schedules match actual use.

Artificial intelligence can help identify unusual patterns in meter readings.
People should investigate each recommendation and verify comfort, safety, and
operational requirements before approving a change.

Measure the result against the baseline. Record the assumptions, keep a rollback
plan, and review whether the improvement continues over time.
